Founding Partner

Raquelle Rogers.

Founding Partner, Biosuite.

Raquelle brings over 20 years of athletic experience to Biosuite as a former competitive tennis player and current tennis pro.

After navigating cancer, a torn bicep, a fused spine, and blood clots in her legs, her perspective on health and wellness was profoundly shaped. Her journey taught her that feeling your best extends far beyond fitness. It encompasses recovery, prevention, longevity, and creating an environment that supports the body’s ability to thrive.

Outside of athletics, Raquelle has a strong appreciation for real estate, architecture, and interior design, and believes that our surroundings play an important role in how we feel, live, and perform.

As Founding Partner of Biosuite, Raquelle is dedicated to making wellness more accessible by reimagining the spaces in which people live, recover, and thrive.
