Questions

FAQ

Where can I put one?

Your local jurisdiction decides. Many places permit tiny homes on wheels as ADUs or as dwellings, and rules vary by state, county, and city.

Unit 1 is headed to San Diego County on a dwelling permit path. We'll work through your address on the design call.

Can I rent it out?

In many places, yes. Rental rules depend on where the home sits: some areas allow nightly stays, others set a minimum stay length.

Confirm your local rules before you count on rental use. We do not project or guarantee rental income, occupancy, or resale value.

What does my site need?

A level pad with access for a single-load delivery, plus power, water, and sewer or septic. We recommend 200A electrical service and a 3/4" water main for a full equipment package; many configurations need less.

Utility hookups need their own permits and a local licensed contractor. We review your site before you commit.

What does it cost?

Price depends on configuration, mostly on the technology you choose. You get a written quote on your design call, before you pay anything beyond the refundable reservation. Your price is set in your build agreement.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Financing is available for qualified buyers, subject to credit approval. We'll walk through options on your design call, and we provide the specification and permitting documents a lender asks for.

What's the warranty?

Each equipment manufacturer warrants its own equipment under its own terms. We handle integration and help coordinate claims. Warranty terms for the home itself are spelled out in your build agreement.

How long does it take?

An estimated 45–60 days to build, plus certification, permitting, and site work, which vary by location. Builds start in reservation order.

Can I move it later?

Yes. It's built on a chassis and travels as a single load. A new location means permitting again under that jurisdiction's rules.

Larger, modular versions are available on request. Those are scoped per project and may travel in more than one load.

What if I can't get a permit?

Ask for your deposit back any time before you sign a build agreement. After that, the build agreement governs.
