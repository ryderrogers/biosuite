FAQ

Where can I put one?

Your local jurisdiction decides, not us. In unincorporated San Diego County, a September 2025 Director's Determination allows a tiny home on wheels that is certified to ANSI A119.5 and meets the County's fire-safety and site requirements to be permitted as a dwelling. Unit 1 is headed to Fallbrook on that path.

The City of San Diego and the City of Los Angeles permit movable tiny houses as ADUs behind an existing home. Elsewhere, rules vary. We'll work through your address on the design call.

Can I rent it out?

Usually yes for stays of 30 days or longer. Nightly rental is restricted or prohibited for ADUs and movable tiny houses in most California jurisdictions, San Diego included.

Confirm your local rules before you count on rental use. We do not project or guarantee rental income, occupancy, or resale value.

What does my site need?

A level pad with access for a single-load delivery, 200A+ electrical service, water on a 3/4" main line, and sewer or septic. Utility hookups need their own permits and a local licensed contractor. We review your site before you commit.

What does it cost?

Price depends on configuration, mostly on the technology you choose. You get a written, itemized quote on your design call, before you pay anything beyond the refundable reservation. Your price is set in your build agreement.

Do you offer financing?

No. We are not a lender and we don't arrange or broker loans. Buyers typically pay cash or use their own lender. We'll provide specification and permitting documents your lender asks for.

What's the warranty?

Each equipment manufacturer warrants its own equipment under its own terms. We handle integration and help coordinate claims. Warranty terms for the home itself are spelled out in your build agreement.

How long does it take?

An estimated 45–60 days to build, plus certification, permitting, and site work, which vary by location. Builds start in reservation order.

Can I move it later?

Yes. It's built on a chassis and travels as a single load. A new location means permitting again under that jurisdiction's rules.

What if I can't get a permit?

Ask for your $1,000 back any time before you sign a build agreement. After that, the build agreement governs.

Is this medical equipment?

No. Biosuite is a home fitted with general-wellness equipment. Nothing here is medical advice or intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
