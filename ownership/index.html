Ownership

Three ways to own one.

Live in it, rent it for longer stays, or place several. Your local rules decide what’s allowed.

Rendering.

Personal use

Live in it.

A guest house, a home studio, or a primary residence, where your jurisdiction allows it.

You configure it on the design call. It stays yours to move later, under the new location’s rules.

30+ day rental

Rent it by the month.

Stays of 30 days or longer are usually allowed. Nightly rental is restricted or prohibited for ADUs and movable tiny houses in most California jurisdictions, San Diego included.

We do not project or guarantee rental income, occupancy, or resale value.

Enterprise & multi-unit

Place several.

For hospitality properties, resort campuses, and corporate wellness programs.

Multi-unit layouts are scoped by consultation.

Permitting

Your jurisdiction decides.

Unincorporated San Diego County

Permit path as a dwelling in unincorporated San Diego County.

A September 2025 Director’s Determination allows a tiny home on wheels certified to ANSI A119.5, meeting the County’s fire-safety and site requirements, to be permitted as a dwelling.

Unit 1 is headed to Fallbrook on that path.

City of San Diego · City of Los Angeles

Both permit movable tiny houses as ADUs behind an existing home.

Everywhere else

Rules vary. We work through your address on the design call.

We can’t promise a permit. If you can’t get one, ask for your $1,000 back any time before you sign a build agreement.
