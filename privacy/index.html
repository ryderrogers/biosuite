Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy covers biosuiteusa.com, run by Human Performance Centers LLC (“we,” “us”). It explains what we collect, why, and who else handles it.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, optional phone number, the options you pick, your message, and the page you sent it from.
  • Reservations: payment is handled by Stripe on its own checkout page. We never see or store your card number. Stripe tells us what we need to manage your reservation: typically your name, email, amount, and payment status.
  • Email, calls, and texts: if you contact us directly, we keep that correspondence.

Why we use it

To reply to you, run your reservation and any refund, prepare your design call and quote, and keep the records we’re required to keep for tax and accounting. Nothing else.

Who processes it

  • Formspree delivers and stores contact-form submissions for us. If it can’t be reached, your mail app opens with the message filled in instead. Formspree’s privacy policy.
  • Stripe processes reservation payments and refunds, and sets its own cookies on its checkout page. Stripe’s privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and may log technical information such as your IP address when you load a page. GitHub’s privacy statement.

Analytics and cookies

This site doesn’t run analytics or advertising trackers, and it doesn’t set cookies. If we add analytics in future, we’ll update this policy before turning it on.

Do Not Track

Because this site doesn’t track you across sites or for advertising, there’s nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off. We treat every visitor the same way whether or not their browser sends one.

We don’t sell your information

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to work with you and to meet tax and accounting obligations. Then we delete it.

Access and deletion

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email ryder@biosuiteusa.com. We’ll respond within 30 days. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it, and we’ll tell you if so.

Children

This site isn’t meant for anyone under 18, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think we have, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We post any change on this page and update the effective date at the top. Material changes take effect when posted.
