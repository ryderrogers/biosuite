Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

This policy covers biosuiteusa.com, run by Human Performance Centers LLC (“we,” “us”). It explains what we collect, why, and who else handles it.

The contact form

When you send the form on our About page, we collect your name, email, optional phone number, the options you pick, your message, and the page you sent it from.

The form is delivered to us by Formspree, a form-processing service, which stores the submission on our behalf. If Formspree can’t be reached, your device’s mail app opens with the message filled in instead.

We use this information only to reply to you and discuss a possible build.

Reservations and payments

Reservation payments are taken on a checkout page hosted by Stripe. Stripe collects your card details; we never see or store your full card number.

Stripe shares with us what we need to manage your reservation and any refund: typically your name, email, amount, and payment status. Stripe’s handling of your data is covered by Stripe’s privacy policy. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout page.

Analytics and cookies

This site does not currently run analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not set cookies.

If we add analytics in future, we’ll update this policy before turning it on.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load a page. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Email and phone

If you email, call, or text us, we keep that correspondence so we can follow up.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and reservation records as long as needed to work with you and to meet tax and accounting obligations, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you. Email ryder@hpcla.com and we’ll respond within 30 days.

Children

This site isn’t directed to children under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
