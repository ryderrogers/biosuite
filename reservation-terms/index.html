Reservation Terms

Last updated November 10, 2026

Who you’re dealing with

Reservations are accepted by Human Performance Centers LLC (“we,” “us”). Reach us at ryder@hpcla.com or 760.695.2876.

What a reservation is

A reservation is a refundable deposit of $1,000. It holds a position in the build queue. Slots fill in the order reservations arrive.

It includes a one-on-one design call with Ryder to configure your home and review your site, and a written, itemized quote before you commit to anything more.

What it isn’t

A reservation is not a purchase agreement or construction contract. It sells no home, fixes no price, conveys no interest in any unit or real property, and obligates neither of us to proceed.

It doesn’t guarantee a delivery date.

Refunds

Your reservation is fully refundable on request any time before you sign a build agreement. You don’t need to give a reason.

To request a refund, email ryder@hpcla.com. The full $1,000 goes back to your original payment method within 10 business days of your request.

If we cancel your reservation, you get the same full refund.

If you sign a build agreement

Price, specifications, and schedule are set only in a signed build agreement. If you sign one, your $1,000 is credited toward the purchase price.

From then on, the build agreement governs, including any refund rights.

Schedule

Delivery dates are estimates that depend on certification, permitting, site readiness, and queue position.

Unit 1 is in production, built to the ANSI A119.5 standard, third-party certification in progress, targeted for delivery to San Diego County this winter.

Permits and your site

Permits are the owner’s responsibility and are decided by your local jurisdiction. We don’t promise that any permit will be granted.

Utility hookups need their own permits and a local licensed contractor.

Rental and value

We do not project or guarantee rental income, occupancy, or resale value.

Payment

Reservation payments are processed by Stripe. We don’t see or store your full card number. See our Privacy Policy.
