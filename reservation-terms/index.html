Reservation Terms

Effective November 13, 2026

These terms apply when you place a $10,000 Biosuite reservation. They’re written to be read, not skimmed past. If anything is unclear, ask before you pay.

1. Who you’re dealing with

Reservations are accepted by Human Performance Centers LLC (“we,” “us”). Contact details are at the end of these terms.

2. Who can reserve

You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter into a binding agreement. You reserve for yourself, under your own name.

3. What a reservation is

A reservation is a refundable deposit of $10,000. It holds a position in the build queue. Slots fill in the order reservations arrive.

It includes a one-on-one design call with Ryder to configure your home and review your site, and a written quote before you commit to anything more.

4. What it isn’t

A reservation is not a purchase agreement or construction contract. It sells no home, fixes no price, conveys no interest in any unit or real property, and obligates neither of us to proceed.

It doesn’t guarantee a delivery date.

5. Refunds

Your reservation is fully refundable on request any time before you sign a build agreement. You don’t need to give a reason.

To request a refund, email ryder@biosuiteusa.com from the address you reserved with. The full $10,000 goes back to your original payment method, through Stripe, within 10 business days of your request. Your bank or card issuer may take a few more days to show it.

If we cancel your reservation for any reason, you get the same full refund on the same timeline.

6. Transfers

Your reservation is personal to you. You can’t sell, assign, or transfer it, or your queue position, to anyone else without our written consent.

7. Estimates are not promises

Build times, delivery dates, queue positions, and any figures we discuss before a build agreement are estimates, not promises. They depend on certification, permitting, site readiness, and queue position.

Unit 1 is in production, built to the ANSI A119.5 standard, third-party certification in progress, targeted for delivery to San Diego County this winter.

8. Permits and your site

Permits are the owner’s responsibility and are decided by your local jurisdiction. We don’t promise that any permit will be granted.

Utility hookups need their own permits and a local licensed contractor.

9. Rental and value

We do not project or guarantee rental income, occupancy, or resale value.

10. If you sign a build agreement

Price, specifications, and schedule are set only in a signed build agreement. If you sign one, your $10,000 is credited toward the purchase price. From then on, the build agreement governs, including any refund rights.

11. Payment

Reservation payments are processed by Stripe. We don’t see or store your full card number. See our Privacy Policy.

12. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. Changes apply only to reservations made after the change is posted here. Your reservation stays under the version in effect when you paid.

13. Whole agreement

These terms, plus any build agreement you later sign, are the whole agreement between us about your reservation. If these terms and a signed build agreement conflict, the build agreement controls.

14. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming. That doesn’t take away any consumer protection you have under the laws of the state where you live.
