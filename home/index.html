Health by design

Materials chosen to limit chemical exposure.

  • Untreated wood inside
  • Grounded linoleum floors, laid with ultra-low-VOC adhesive
  • Blackout windows for full darkness at night
  • Circadian lighting that follows the day
  • Hydrogen water on tap

No home is free of every chemical. These are the choices we make on purpose.

Site recommendations

What we recommend for your site.

  • A level pad with access for a single-load delivery
  • 200A electrical service (many configurations need less)
  • A 3/4" water main (smaller lines work for lighter setups)
  • Sewer or septic

What you need depends on the equipment you choose. Utility hookups need their own permits and a local licensed contractor. We review your site before you commit.
